🩺 Diabetes & Insulin

Optional · clinical worksheet
Safety first: This module displays prescribed ratios and educational starting estimates, not a recommended insulin dose. Do not administer or change insulin based on this tool. Confirm insulin regimen, active insulin, glucose trend, meal content, and local clinical protocol.

If TDD is entered it takes priority; otherwise basal + bolus are summed. 500/1800 rules for rapid-acting insulin; 450/1500 rules for regular insulin. Estimates are not prescriptions.

ISF · glucose fall per unit—Awaiting confirmed factor
Meal carbohydrate coverage (math only)—g carbohydrate ÷ ICR (not a dose recommendation)
Enter clinician-confirmed parameters and verified meal carbohydrate grams. No correction dose is generated.
A glucose correction should be determined using the clinician's individualized protocol, accounting for insulin on board, timing, illness and CGM trends.

Calculations use adult educational rules only. Not suitable as a pump bolus calculator, automated insulin dosing system, or for independent use in children, pregnancy, acute illness, ketosis, or unstable kidney function. A low glucose reading requires hypoglycemia protocol, not correction insulin. The printable report labels all estimates clearly.
